The studio

BLKRØM is an independent creative studio based in Perth, specialising in editorial photography, cinematic film and selective creator management.

We create distinctive visuals for individuals, creators and brands — bringing together creative direction, atmosphere and a strong sense of identity.

From a single production to a long-term partnership, the focus is on creating something that feels unmistakably yours.

Photography

Editorial portraits, personal branding and considered imagery. We use light, composition and styling to create photographs with character, atmosphere and individuality.

Film

Cinematic reels, short films and visual campaigns. From the first frame to the final cut, we bring movement, emotion and atmosphere together to tell stories worth watching.

Creator management

Selective partnerships for creators looking beyond individual productions. We bring content, positioning and long-term creative direction together to build a distinctive and consistent presence.

The founder

Fabian Hornung

I built BLKRØM around what I enjoy most: creating photographs and films that have a mood, a point of view and a reason to exist.

I'm drawn to the details that change how an image feels: where the light falls, how someone moves, what stays in the frame and what doesn't. I don't believe every person needs the same look, or every project needs the same formula.

Photography and film are at the heart of BLKRØM. Creator management is a longer-term extension of that approach, bringing visual direction, content and brand development together without losing what makes someone individual.
